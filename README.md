# Roman Bharti — 3D Interactive Portfolio

An immersive **3D Developer & Engineering Portfolio Website** customized for **Roman Bharti**, Chemical Engineering Student at NIT Raipur.

Built with **React**, **Three.js**, **React Three Fiber (R3F)**, **React Three Drei**, **Framer Motion 3D**, and **Tailwind CSS**.

- **Institution**: National Institute of Technology (NIT) Raipur
- **Specializations**: Process Simulation (Aspen Plus), Chemical Reaction Engineering, Thermodynamics, Heat & Mass Transfer, and Computational Engineering

---

## 🌟 3D Scene Features

- **Interactive 3D Avatar & Office Room**: Fully modeled 3D room with custom lighting, materials, and physics.
- **Scroll-Controlled Camera**: Smooth section-by-section camera navigation powered by Drei's `ScrollControls`.
- **Dynamic 3D Project Gallery**: Interactive 3D project planes rendered on the canvas that users can click to open project links.
- **Animated Skill Bars & Interactive Contact Form**: Responsive 3D and 2D UI layers seamlessly blended.

---

## 📁 Key Files to Update Links & Details

1. **Personal Bio & Skills**:
   - [`src/components/Interface.jsx`](./src/components/Interface.jsx): Contains the headline, introduction, skills progress bars, and social links.
2. **Projects & Showcase**:
   - [`src/components/Projects.jsx`](./src/components/Projects.jsx): Update project titles, descriptions, and URLs in the `projects` array.
3. **Contact & Email Service**:
   - [`src/components/EmailFrom.jsx`](./src/components/EmailFrom.jsx): Set up your EmailJS service ID, template ID, and public key to receive direct messages.
4. **Resume**:
   - [`src/components/Menu.jsx`](./src/components/Menu.jsx) & [`src/components/Interface.jsx`](./src/components/Interface.jsx): Replace the `#` link with your Google Drive resume URL.
